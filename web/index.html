Methodology, data scope & limitations

Data: 2021 NFL, Weeks 1–8, 122 games, a sample of passing dropbacks. Every play is a dropback — this describes behavior on sampled dropbacks, not all offensive plays, and there are no designed run plays.

Unit of analysis: one player on one play. Tracking frames are not counted as independent samples.

Alignment from PFF snap labels (validated against tracking geometry); route vs block from PFF role (the model's target, never an input).

Model: pre-snap inputs only, trained on weeks 1–5 and evaluated on weeks 6–8, with a leakage-free historical feature. Observed facts, held-out predictions, and coaching hypotheses are kept distinct throughout.

Not claimed: nearest defender ≠ assigned coverage; net play yards ≠ individual receiving yards; no coverage/assignment is claimed to be proven effective.
